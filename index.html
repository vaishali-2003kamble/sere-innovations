The problem small farmers face

Many small poultry farmers produce eggs but still rely on external hatcheries for chicks. That dependence creates real headaches at the farm gate.

  • Higher costs — fees, transport, and middle steps eat into margins that are already tight.
  • Less control — you work around someone else’s schedule, not your farm’s natural rhythm.
  • Delays and uncertainty — waiting on batches can slow growth plans or leave you short when demand picks up.
  • Reduced self-reliance — the farm stays one step removed from a core part of the value chain.

What Sere Innovations offers

We design smart incubation systems priced around ₹10,000—built so ordinary farmers can hatch their own chicks without needing an engineering background.

The incubator keeps temperature and humidity in a healthy range, turns eggs automatically, and keeps operation straightforward. You get local control over hatching cycles and a clearer path to steady income—without the constant back-and-forth to commercial hatcheries.

Our focus is practical technology at the grassroots: useful, understandable, and grounded in how farms actually run.

How this addresses the gap

  1. You keep eggs on the farm and run batches when it suits you.
  2. The system handles environment basics so you’re not guessing day and night.
  3. You reduce outside dependency and keep more of the value close to home.
